About the meanings and checks you see.

Babinom shows etymological meanings, origins, and translations for the names in its catalog, and can check how a name may be heard in other languages. Much of this content is produced or assisted by artificial intelligence. This page explains what that means and how to interpret what you see.

Effective date: June 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

What is AI-generated in the App

Meanings. English meanings for names not in our curated dictionary were produced by AI, as were the translations of those meanings into the other languages the App supports (Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Modern Standard Arabic). The large majority of the meanings shown in Babinom are produced or translated by an AI model. The rest are editorially curated by us or sourced from public-domain references such as Wikidata.

Name checks. The notes that tell you how a name may be heard in other languages — a meaning, a nickname, a rhyme, or a word it sounds like — are produced by an AI model. For names in our catalog they are generated in advance; when you check a name you typed, or a full name with your surname, the check runs at that moment.

Rhythm hint. The note about how a first name and surname flow together is a simple rule of thumb based on general research on speech rhythm, not on studies of names. It is not AI-generated.

Each meaning and flag in our database records where it came from — curated, a public-domain source, or the AI model that produced it — so we always know which content came from where.

How accurate is AI-generated content

AI language models produce plausible-sounding text. They are not authoritative reference works.

Specifically, meanings may be incomplete, oversimplified, or one-sided. Origins may be assigned to the most common tradition when multiple cultures use the same name. Translations may be grammatically correct but culturally unidiomatic. Religious, mythological, or historical references may be summarised in ways that experts in those traditions would refine or contest. Rare or recently-coined names may produce confident-sounding but invented etymologies.

We mark each meaning with the model's self-assessed confidence; meanings below a threshold are flagged in our database for human review.

What a name check can and cannot tell you

A name check is information, not a verdict and not a clearance. It is a quick look at how a name may be heard, produced by an AI model. It is not a guarantee that a name is free of unwanted meanings, and it is not a substitute for asking people who speak the language.

Specifically:

  • It only covers the languages it checks. Babinom checks a limited set of languages (currently up to twelve, depending on your settings). A name can carry a meaning in any other language, dialect, or community that we do not check at all.
  • It can miss things. Slang, regional and dialect usage, brand names, public figures, internet culture, and associations within particular communities are often not known to an AI model, or not known reliably.
  • Language changes. A word or association can appear, spread, or change meaning after the check was run. A result reflects what the model produced at that moment, and we do not re-check or notify you later.
  • It can also be wrong the other way. It may flag a meaning that is rare, outdated, or not how most people would hear the name.
  • “Nothing came up” means nothing was found — not that nothing exists. The App says so on the screen: “That is not a guarantee.”
  • It says nothing about the law. A name check does not tell you whether a civil registry in your country will accept a name.

Choosing a name is your decision, and yours alone. Babinom gives you information to consider; it does not advise you to choose or avoid any name, and it cannot know how a name will be received in your family, community, or the future. If how a name is heard in a particular language or community matters to you, ask people who speak that language or belong to that community before you decide.

If you know of a meaning or saying a check missed, you can suggest it in the App. Suggestions are reviewed by us before they appear.

What you should NOT use it for

Please do not rely on the meanings or name checks shown in Babinom as the sole basis for choosing a name, or for legal naming, birth-certificate, or registry decisions; religious naming traditions where specific scriptural or community guidance matters; genealogical research where source provenance is required; academic linguistic or historical work; or tattoos, family heirlooms, and other permanent commitments where accuracy matters.

For any of the above, please consult an authoritative reference such as a published etymological dictionary, religious authority, or qualified naming specialist.

Low-temperature generation

We generate meanings with settings that favour consistency over creativity.

Confidence flagging

The model self-rates each meaning and each name-check flag. Low-confidence meanings are flagged for review, and low-confidence name-check flags are not shown.

Output validation

We validate every output against basic format rules (length, no refusal phrases, no tautologies) before applying.

Source priority

Where both exist for the same name, human-curated and Wikidata-sourced meanings take precedence over AI-generated ones.

How to report an error

If you find a meaning or name check that's wrong, misleading, or culturally insensitive, please email us at support@babinom.com with the name, what you saw, and what's wrong with it. We review reports and update the catalog when justified.

Our use of AI providers

We currently use two language-model providers.

OpenAI generates and translates meanings. This happens on our servers before anything reaches your device. The names we send are names already in our catalog, not data tied to your account.

Anthropic runs name checks. Checks for catalog names are generated in advance from catalog names only. When you ask for a live check, our server — never your phone directly — sends Anthropic the name you typed and, if you check a full name, the surname or surnames saved for your room, together with the languages to check. We do not send your email address, account details, or anything else about you. Anthropic processes this as our service provider; see our Privacy Policy for details, including the transfer outside the EEA.

For information on what data is collected and processed in connection with your use of Babinom, see our Privacy Policy.
