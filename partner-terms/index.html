Partner Terms

Working together, in plain words.

These terms apply to creators, publishers and other partners (“you”) who promote Babinom in exchange for a commission, a fee or free access. They sit alongside the email in which we agree your deal.

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Leer en español

1. How a deal is made

We agree the specifics of each collaboration by email: your link name, the commission and any discount for your audience, any fixed fee, and the start and end dates. When you reply that you accept, the deal consists of that email plus these terms. If the email and these terms differ, the email wins.

Babinom is operated by the business identified in our Terms of Service. You must be at least 18 to take part. Collaborations are not exclusive: you are free to work with other brands.

2. Your link and your code

You get a personal link, babinom.com/<your-name>, and a private dashboard showing visits, sales and commission. Keep the dashboard link to yourself: anyone holding it can see your figures.

A sale is credited to you when we can attribute it to you: purchases made on babinom.com through your link, and, once the app supports it, App Store and Google Play purchases by people who arrived through your link or entered your creator code. Your own purchases, purchases by your household, and purchases made only to earn commission are not credited.

3. Commission

Commission is a percentage of net revenue: the price the buyer paid, minus VAT, payment-processor or app-store fees, and any refund or chargeback. If your deal includes a discount for your audience, the discount and your commission come from one shared budget, as agreed in your email.

Commission applies to sales made between the start and end dates in your email. A sale counts once it is 30 days old and has not been refunded. If a credited sale is refunded later, we may deduct it from a future payment.

4. Payment

We pay monthly, by bank transfer, by the 15th of the month for the previous month's confirmed sales. Amounts under €20 roll over to the next month, and whatever is owed is always paid when the collaboration ends. We will ask for your full name, address, tax ID (NIF/DNI) and bank details.

We prepare the invoice for you. By accepting these terms you agree that Babinom issues each invoice for your commission in your name (self-billing, marked “Facturación por el destinatario”). We send it to you with the payment; if you do not object within 15 days, it counts as accepted.

Tax. If you are registered as self-employed (autónomo) in Spain, 21% Spanish VAT is added on top of your commission, and if your registration requires it, income-tax withholding (IRPF) is deducted and paid to the tax office on your behalf. If you are not registered, no VAT is charged; where the law requires it we withhold IRPF and report the payment to the tax office. Partners outside Spain are paid without Spanish VAT or withholding unless the law says otherwise. You remain responsible for your own tax obligations, including whether you need to register. You act as an independent partner, not as an employee or agent of Babinom.

5. Free Premium

If we give you and your partner free Premium so you can make your content, it is yours to keep, including after the collaboration ends. It is not transferable and has no cash value.

6. Honest content

You make your content your way. Because you receive something in return (a commission, a fee or free Premium), your posts about Babinom are advertising under Spanish law and the Spanish influencer advertising code (AUTOCONTROL, 2025), even when you only share your link. So we ask for these things:

  • Label it as advertising, at the start. Switch on the platform's own label (on TikTok “Paid partnership” / “Promotional content”) and write a clear label in the language of the post at the beginning: in Spanish “publi”, “publicidad” or “colaboración pagada con Babinom”; in English “ad” or “paid partnership with Babinom”. Not hidden among other hashtags or behind “more”, and not an English “#ad” on a Spanish post.
  • Only say what is true. Do not claim you chose your child's name with Babinom unless you did, and do not invent features, prices or results.
  • Children on screen. If your children appear, keep it appropriate for their age and respect their privacy; you are responsible for the consent of anyone who appears in your content.
  • AI. If the content is created or substantially changed with AI, say so where the law or the platform requires it, and never in a way that misleads.
  • Follow the rules of the platform you post on and the advertising law that applies to you.
  • No spam, no fake activity: no buying views or followers, no misleading ads that bid on the name “Babinom”, no posting your link where it is not welcome.

If a post is missing its label or says something factually wrong, we will ask you to correct it, and you agree to do so promptly.

7. Using your content

Your content stays yours. You allow us to share or repost it on Babinom's own social accounts and website, with credit to you and with its advertising label kept. Using it in paid advertising, such as TikTok Spark Ads, needs your separate written consent, which we will ask for and may pay for.

8. Your data

We use your name, contact details and payment details only to run the collaboration and to meet our accounting and tax obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy. You never receive personal data about buyers; your dashboard shows totals only.

9. Ending the collaboration

Either of us can end the collaboration at any time by email. Commission on sales already made is still paid. After the end date your link keeps working for visitors, but new sales are no longer credited unless we agree an extension. We may end it immediately, without commission on the affected sales, if these terms are seriously breached (for example fake purchases or misleading content).

10. Changes and law

We may update these terms and will tell you by email at least 30 days before a change takes effect. A change never affects a running deal unless you agree to it. Spanish law applies. These terms exist in English and Spanish, and both are equally valid; if they differ, the language of your deal email prevails.

11. Contact

Questions about a collaboration: partnerships@babinom.com.
